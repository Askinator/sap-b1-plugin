# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-28_

This policy covers the **SAP Business One plugin for Claude** (`sap-b1`) published from
<https://github.com/Askinator/sap-b1-plugin>.

## What the plugin is

The plugin consists only of instructions (markdown skills) that Claude reads. It contains no
executable code, no bundled MCP server, no analytics, and no telemetry. The plugin author does not
operate any service that receives data from the plugin.

## What data is collected by the plugin author

None. The plugin author does not collect, receive, store, or share any personal data, business
data, or usage data through the plugin.

## Where your data goes when you use it

When you use the plugin, Claude works with your SAP Business One data through an MCP connector
that **you or your organization** add and configure (Settings → Connectors). That means:

- **Your SAP B1 data** (business partners, documents, accounts, attachments you choose to upload)
  travels between Claude and the SAP B1 MCP server your organization hosts. How that server
  processes and stores data is governed by your organization and whoever operates that server.
- **Your conversations with Claude** are processed by Anthropic under the terms and privacy policy
  that apply to your Claude plan (for example, your organization's Claude Team or Enterprise
  agreement).

The plugin does not send data anywhere else.
